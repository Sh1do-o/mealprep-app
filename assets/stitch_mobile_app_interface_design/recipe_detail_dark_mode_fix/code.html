Dorm-Room Tuna Rice Bowl

schedule 10 min microwave Microwave Easy

Ingredients

Macros per serving

Protein 28g
Carbs 45g

Cooking Steps

1

Heat Rice

Microwave the pre-cooked rice packet according to package instructions (usually 90 seconds). Careful, it will be hot!

2

Mix Tuna

Drain the canned tuna and transfer it to a bowl. Mix in the soy sauce and half of the sriracha mayo until well combined.

3

Assemble

Place the hot rice in your main bowl. Top with the tuna mixture. Drizzle the remaining sriracha mayo over the top and garnish with sesame seeds if you have them.
